This policy applies to all orders with **{{legal_name}}**. A signed Order may set different terms for that Order.

## 1. Payments

- Prices are in Indian Rupees; GST and other applicable taxes are charged extra as shown on the tax invoice.
- Payment is due by the date on the invoice. Pay by bank transfer, UPI or cheque to the account printed on our invoice only. **We never ask you to pay into a personal account.** If in doubt, call us on {{phone}} before paying.
- Usage-based services (minutes, messages, credits) are prepaid or billed as stated in the Order.
- Late payment may lead to suspension and interest at 18% per annum.

## 2. Cancellation

- **Before work starts:** you may cancel an Order in writing; we refund advance payments less any amounts already paid to third parties (Carriers, publishers, manufacturers).
- **Subscriptions and monthly services:** cancel with 30 days' written notice before the next billing date. Charges for the current period are not refunded.
- **Setup, installation, customisation and consulting** are non-refundable once work has started.

## 3. Non-refundable items

- Telecom and platform charges paid to Carriers or platform owners: number, DLT, header, trunk, WhatsApp, SMS and RCS charges, and used credits.
- Software licences once issued or activated (subject only to the publisher's own refund rules).
- Domain, hosting or cloud resources already provisioned.
- Services suspended or terminated because of a breach of our [Terms](/terms/) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy/).

## 4. Hardware

- Check goods on delivery and report damage, shortage or a wrong item within 48 hours with photos.
- Dead-on-arrival items are replaced as per the manufacturer's DOA policy. After that, the manufacturer's warranty applies.
- Goods once sold are not taken back unless they are defective, wrong or approved in writing for return. Returned goods must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories.

## 5. Rentals

Rental charges are billed in advance for the rental period. Equipment must be returned in working condition; damage or loss is charged at repair or replacement cost. The security deposit, if any, is refunded within 15 days of return after deductions.

## 6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment source or the Client's business bank account within 15 working days of approval. We adjust taxes by issuing a credit note as required under GST law.
