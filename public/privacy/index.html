Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Agile Tech Experts Private Limited

This policy explains how Agile Tech Experts Private Limited ("Agile", "we") handles personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. For data our Clients process through our platforms (their contact lists, call recordings and similar), see the Data Processing Terms.

1. What we collect

  • Inquiries and leads: name, phone number, email, company, the service you are interested in and your message — from forms, the chat assistant, the voice assistant, WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Website chat and voice assistant: the text of your chat messages, and for voice calls the audio you speak during the call and the transcript of the conversation, any rating or feedback you give, and basic call details (time, duration, device type). Audio is processed in real time to generate the reply; we store the transcript, not the audio.
  • Clients and portal users: account details, KYC documents of the business and authorised signatory, billing and GST details, invoices and payments, support tickets, and activity logs.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information, pages visited and security logs.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your inquiry, call you back, give demos and quotes, and provide the services you ask for.
  • To verify Clients (KYC) and meet telecom, tax and legal obligations.
  • To bill, collect payments and keep accounts.
  • To check and improve the quality of our AI assistant's answers (staff review conversation transcripts).
  • To keep our website and systems secure and prevent misuse and fraud.

We process data on the basis of your consent, or for legitimate uses permitted by law (for example, to comply with law or to perform a service you requested). We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Calls and messages from us

If you share your number, we may call, SMS, WhatsApp or email you about your inquiry. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP, telling the caller, or writing to info@agiletechexpert.com.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who process data for us: cloud hosting (servers in India and Australia today), AI providers that run the chat and voice assistant (such as Google and NVIDIA), email and messaging providers, and payment and accounting tools. They may process data outside India; they are bound to use it only to provide their service to us.
  • Carriers and platform owners when needed to set up a Client's numbers, headers or connectivity.
  • Authorities when required by law, a court order, or a lawful request from a regulator or law-enforcement agency.

5. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries and leads: up to 3 years after the last contact, unless you ask us to delete them earlier.
  • Chat and voice transcripts: up to 1 year, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Client, KYC, billing and tax records: for as long as the law requires (tax records generally 8 years; telecom-related records as required by Carrier and DoT rules).
  • Security and access logs: at least 1 year.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access a summary of your data, correct or update it, erase it (where we are not required to keep it), withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Write to info@agiletechexpert.com. We reply within the time the law sets and in any case within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, access controls with individual logins and roles, password hashing, firewalls, activity logs and regular updates. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected people and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

8. Cookies

The public website does not use advertising cookies. Portals use essential cookies to keep you signed in. If we add analytics (such as Google Analytics), it will be used only to understand site usage.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data of anyone under 18.
