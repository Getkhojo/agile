FAQ

Common questions

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. We work only with the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Can we use our existing number?

Yes, if it is not active on the WhatsApp app, or after it is moved off the app.

Can WhatsApp connect to the voice platform?

Yes. Missed calls, IVR choices and AI calls can trigger WhatsApp messages.
