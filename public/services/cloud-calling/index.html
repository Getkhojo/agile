On-Premise Setup

Install it in your office or data center

We set up the platform on your servers, inside your network. Your data and recordings never leave your premises, and you can run it under your own brand.

  • Installed and configured by our engineers on site or remotely

Managed Service

We host it and run it for you

Use the service directly from us. No servers to buy, nothing to install. Your team logs in and starts working.

  • Hosted on our cloud with telecom connectivity included
  • 24/7 monitoring and support
  • Client panel with reports, recordings and billing
  • Move to on-premise later without changing platforms
FAQ

Common questions

Do agents need special hardware?

No. A browser with a headset is enough. IP phones are supported if you prefer them.

Can we move from our current PBX?

Yes. We can port or forward your numbers and run both side by side during the switch.

Is the dialer available on-premise?

Yes. The same dialer can run on your servers inside your office.
