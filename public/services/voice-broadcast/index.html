Voice Broadcast & Bulk Voice Calls (OBD) Platform

Reach large lists in minutes with recorded or text-to-speech voice messages. Callers can press a key to talk to an agent, confirm, or opt out, and every response is reported live.

What's included

Voice Broadcast features

High CPS dialing

Dial thousands of calls per minute with pacing per trunk.

Trunk failover and DID rotation

Calls move to a backup trunk automatically, and caller IDs rotate to improve answer rates.

Keypad responses

Capture DTMF input such as 1 to confirm, 2 to talk to an agent, 9 to opt out.

Scheduling and retries

Schedule campaigns by time window and retry unanswered numbers.

White-label reseller panel

Brand the panel as your own and bill your clients monthly.

Live reports

Answered, busy, failed and DTMF results per campaign, exportable.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I resell the voice broadcast panel?

Yes. You get your own branding, an install key and a go-live key bound to your server, and you bill your clients directly.

Is it compliant with TRAI rules?

We set up DND scrubbing and calling-window limits; content approval stays with the sender.

How many calls can it handle?

It depends on your trunk capacity; the platform paces calls per trunk and scales with hardware.
