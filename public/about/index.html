About us

Communication platforms, built by an IT infrastructure team

Agile Tech Experts Pvt. Ltd. started as an IT solutions company supplying and supporting servers, networking, telephony hardware and business software for offices across the NCR.

Year after year, our customers' most critical system was the one that handled their calls. So we went deeper: dialers, IVRs, gateways and PBXs, and now AI voice agents and business messaging on WhatsApp, SMS and RCS.

Today we deliver these platforms two ways: installed on the customer's own premises, or run for them as a managed service. The same engineers handle both.

How we work

What you can expect from us

Reliability first

Voice systems can't go down at 11 a.m. on a Monday. We build with failover and monitor around the clock.

One accountable team

Hardware, software, telecom and support from the same people, so problems don't bounce between vendors.

Your premises, your data

When the system lives in your office, your data stays in your network and under your control.

Practical engineering

We deploy what fits your volumes and budget, and explain the trade-offs plainly.

In-house & managed

At your office, or from ours

Some clients want the system inside their own office. Others want to use it directly from us. We provide both.

On-Premise Setup

Install it in your office or data center

We set up the platform on your servers, inside your network. Your data and recordings never leave your premises, and you can run it under your own brand.

  • Installed and configured by our engineers on site or remotely
  • Works with your SIP trunks, PRI lines and GSM gateways
  • White-label branding for resellers
  • Training for your team and annual support
Managed Service

We host it and run it for you

Use the service directly from us. No servers to buy, nothing to install. Your team logs in and starts working.

  • Hosted on our cloud with telecom connectivity included
  • 24/7 monitoring and support
  • Client panel with reports, recordings and billing
  • Move to on-premise later without changing platforms
